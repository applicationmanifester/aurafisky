About Aurafisky

Aurafisky is an interactive, privacy-first weather explorer that blends live mapping, radar animation, and procedural audio.

✨ Core Features

🗺️
Interactive Map: Click or tap anywhere on the map to load real-time temperature, description, and detailed metrics.
📡
Live Radar Overlay: Toggle the radar overlay from the control panel to see precipitation and wind patterns.
⏱️
Time-Travel Slider: Drag the slider in the bottom timeline drawer to preview forecasted conditions for up to 7 days ahead.
🎵
Ambient Soundscapes: Procedural audio generators play realistic weather sounds (rain, wind, thunder) matched to the weather conditions.
📱
PWA Offline Mode: Add to Home Screen to use the app offline. Recent queries and map layers are automatically saved in your browser storage.

🚀 How to Use

  1. Type a city or region in the search bar at the top, or directly click any point on the globe map.
  2. Explore real-time data panels showing Air Quality Index (AQI), Comfort levels, and detailed parameter charts.
  3. Click the bottom timeline to expand it and scrub through the temporal changes.
  4. Open Settings (gear icon) to switch units or adjust ambient volume.

Version 20.2.0 · No API keys, no tracking, and no ads. Weather data provided by Open-Meteo under CC BY 4.0.

Welcome to Aurafisky

Essential guide to key weather layers & features

🧭 Terrain Elevation Relief vs Temperature

Green, yellow, and red colors on the base map denote topographic elevation (sea level to mountain peaks), not heat. Live temperature is shown on the weather card, temperature grid, and timeline scrubber.

🌧️ Live Radar & 10-Day Timeline Scrubber

Drag the bottom slider to travel through time: view past 3 days and forecast 7 days in 15-minute intervals. Toggle the Radar button to see precipitation and satellite cloud movement.

🛣️ Route Weather Studio

Plan trips across multiple waypoints with real-time weather forecasts sampled along your road path. Departure time adjustments show weather when you will actually arrive.

🤖 Ask Aura Live Intelligence

Tap the top Aura button or Generate Briefing to receive atmospheric insights, best outdoor windows, crop sowing guides, and astronomy star visibility.

Terms & Privacy

Aurafisky is an interactive, privacy-first weather explorer that blends real-time forecasting, live radar animations, and interactive mapping in a modern, user-friendly interface.

Data Sources

  • Weather: Open-Meteo
  • Maps: OpenStreetMap + OpenFreeMap
  • Radar: RainViewer
  • Geocoding: Nominatim

Privacy

No personal data is collected. Location is only used for weather queries and never stored on servers. Favorites & settings sync via Firebase Auth (Google) only if you sign in. For more details, view our Full Privacy Policy.

Offline Use

Install as PWA for full offline access. Recent weather data cached in IndexedDB.
