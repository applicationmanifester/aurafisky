About Aurafisky

Overview

Aurafisky is a weather visualization app that gives you hyperlocal forecasts, air quality data, radar imagery, and climate insights on an interactive map. Click any location or search for a city to get started.

Getting Started

  • Search — Type a city in the search bar at the top and select from suggestions.
  • Click the map — Click any point on the map to see weather data for that location.
  • My Location — Use the • button to jump to your current location.
  • Timeline slider — Drag the slider at the bottom to explore hourly forecasts.

Weather Panel

  • View current conditions, temperature, humidity, wind, pressure, UV index, and more.
  • Hourly & Daily — Scroll hourly strip or daily forecast cards.
  • Air Quality (AQI) — Real-time pollution metrics and pollen counts.
  • Radar overlay — Toggle precipitation and temperature layers on the map.
  • Best Outdoor Window — Suggested time slot with good weather.
  • Share — Share weather details via your device's share sheet.
  • Favorites — Save locations for quick access via the star icon.

Map Controls

  • Zoom — Use the + / − buttons on the right side of the screen, or scroll the mouse wheel.
  • Radar — Toggle weather radar from the floating controls. Choose between precipitation, temperature, or both.
  • Bookmarks — Quickly revisit your saved locations from the star dropdown.

Advanced Features

  • Time Machine — Scroll through historical weather data for any location to see past conditions.
  • Climate Briefing — Generate an AI-powered summary of recent weather trends and anomalies.
  • Compare — Compare any saved location side-by-side with your current active location by clicking the Compare button next to it in the Saved Locations dropdown (star icon).
  • Auto-Refresh — Keep weather data up to date automatically (configurable in Settings).
  • Dark Mode — Switch between light and dark themes in Settings.
  • Compact Mode — Reduce the weather panel to a minimal view showing only essentials.
  • Soundscapes — Ambient audio that reflects current weather conditions.
  • Cloud Sync — Sign in with Google to sync your settings and favorites across devices.
